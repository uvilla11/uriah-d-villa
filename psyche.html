Hephaestus landing system

A lander concept needed repeatable indexing of a rotating rover platform to six exit positions. I led electronics and firmware integration within an ASU capstone through Psyche Student Collaborations.

Problem

A six-door deployment platform requires a repeatable reference for rotary alignment. The sensor and motor-driver circuitry must keep delivering usable signals after mechanical and PCB revisions.

Role and team outcome

My role

Led the electronics and firmware subsystem: custom PCB, Hall-effect position sensing, and motor-control integration.

Team outcome

The ASU team designed and built the broader lander prototype. The official Psyche capstone listing names Uriah Villa among the robotics students; it does not independently certify subsystem test figures.

Constraint and decision

Use Hall-effect feedback to establish rotary alignment rather than assuming that a motor command proves position. Integrate sensing, signal conditioning, and the motor driver on the controller board.

Implementation

Integrated a PIC18/PSoC-controlled positioning system with a custom PCB and six exit positions. My project reporting describes a board revision for motor-driver and Hall-signal conditioning issues. The retained poster shows PCB work, but does not establish the driver failure mechanism or revision chronology.

Validation

±2°project-reported rotation accuracy across six destinationsSource: my supplied project reporting. The setup, load, instrument, and acceptance definition are not recorded here. It also reports 500+ automated cycles, but does not establish that this accuracy held in every cycle. The official listing does not certify either figure.

The team's prototype was also evaluated on an incline and through structural simulation. Those team-level mechanical results are distinct from my electronics and firmware contribution.

Limits

This was an academic proof of concept, not a NASA flight-qualified system. The 500+ cycles and ±2° figures come from project reporting. The official Psyche listing establishes team membership and scope, not those quantitative results.
